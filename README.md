This is a wizard farming game, inspired by a certain game involving stars, dew, and a valley. Currently it is just a demo, as a proof-of-concept.
The main gimmick is the ability to craft spells. Currently it just has six, two of which you start with and four being craftable.
HOW TO MAKE A SPELL
1: Plant a crop, and harvest it.
2: Extract the crop's elemental essence.
3: Take it to the orb, to turn the essence into a spell scroll.
4: Add the scroll to your spellbook.
